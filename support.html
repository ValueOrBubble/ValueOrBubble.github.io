I bought the full game and changed phones. How do I get it back?

Open ⚙️ Settings in the game and tap “Restore purchases” with the same Apple ID.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device, and in your iCloud account if you are signed in to Game Center, so your progress comes back automatically if you delete the game or change phones. The full game can be restored from Settings.

What is free and what is paid?

The rented room and the studio are free. The full game is a single purchase with no subscription and no ads; hint 💡 packs are optional.

Are the companies real?

No. Companies are fictional and results are simulated for education. This is not investment advice.

How do I change the language or turn on dark mode?

In ⚙️ Settings inside the game.
